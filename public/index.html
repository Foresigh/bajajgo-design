The Problem

Bajaj rides in Ethiopia are broken

😤
Endless price negotiation
Every single trip starts with a haggle. Unfair pricing, especially for newcomers and tourists.
⏱
No way to find a Bajaj nearby
You stand on the road and hope one passes. No visibility, no tracking, no ETA.
🛡
Zero safety accountability
No driver identity, no trip records, no way to report issues. Passengers — especially women — feel unsafe.
